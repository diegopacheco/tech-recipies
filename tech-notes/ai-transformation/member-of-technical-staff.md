# Member of Technical Staff (MTS)

## What is the trend: "Member of Technical Staff"

The "Member of Technical Staff" (MTS) trend is a shift where senior engineering leaders—people who were previously VPs, Directors, and CTOs at Big Tech companies (Meta, Netflix, Google, Amazon, Microsoft)—are leaving executive management to take Individual Contributor (IC) roles with the MTS title at frontier AI companies. Instead of climbing the traditional management ladder to increase their scope, they are moving to flat organizations to directly build core technologies on the front lines.

## What is obvious

*   **Desire to build**: High-level managers want to write code and build products again rather than spending their days in administrative meetings and managing spreadsheets.
*   **AI prestige**: Working at frontier AI labs is currently the most prestigious opportunity in tech, making it worth dropping an executive title.
*   **Flat hierarchies**: Fast-moving startups naturally prefer flat hierarchies with fewer management layers to maintain high engineering velocity.
*   **Compensation equivalence**: Top AI companies are paying ICs compensation packages that match or exceed Big Tech executive salaries, removing the financial penalty for dropping a VP title.

## What is non-obvious

*   **AI provides executive-level leverage to ICs**: Because of AI coding assistants and automation, a single high-impact IC can now produce the output that previously required coordinating a whole team. The leverage moved from managing headcount to utilizing AI tools.
*   **Title obfuscation as defense**: Using a unified MTS title across the organization acts as strategic opacity. It makes it extremely difficult for competitors to map the internal org chart or poach specific leaders, as everyone externally has the exact same title.
*   **End of the traditional career ladder**: This trend signals that "management" is no longer the default promotion for elite engineers. The highest status in the AI era is technical execution, effectively decoupling prestige from the number of direct reports.
*   **Egalitarian culture filter**: Forcing former VPs to take the exact same title as new hires acts as an ego filter during the hiring process, ensuring that the people joining are driven purely by the work and not by status symbols.

## Pros / Cons

### Pros
*   **High velocity**: Teams ship much faster without the overhead of middle management and bureaucratic approval processes.
*   **Talent density**: Attracts elite technical talent who want to remain hands-on without feeling penalized in status or compensation.
*   **Resource efficiency**: Capital is spent directly on high-output builders rather than coordination layers.

### Cons
*   **Management vacuum**: Flat structures can suffer from a lack of mentorship for junior engineers and poor handling of interpersonal conflicts.
*   **Ambiguous progression**: Without traditional titles (Senior, Staff, Principal, Director), engineers may struggle to understand their career progression or prove their seniority externally.
*   **Overload risk**: High-impact ICs might burn out faster if they are forced to handle both deep technical work and the inevitable organizational communication that managers usually absorb.
