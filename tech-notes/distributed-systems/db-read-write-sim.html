How a row travels through memory and disk on the way in and out. Pick an engine, then play a write or a read and watch the sequence light up step by step. Relational (PostgreSQL / MySQL-InnoDB) rides a B+tree and updates pages in place; Cassandra rides an LSM-tree and only ever appends.
