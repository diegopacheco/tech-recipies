Geospatial Indexes

Two dimensions have no total order, so a B-tree cannot index them directly. Every structure here solves that in one of two ways: cut the plane into a tree of boxes, or flatten the plane onto a curve and index the resulting integer. Pick a structure and step the search to see what each one actually reads.
